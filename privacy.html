This Privacy Policy explains how Gmail Manager (“the app”) handles information when you use this browser-based Gmail management website.

2. Google account authorization

When you choose to connect a Google account, the app uses Google Identity Services to obtain an OAuth access token and calls the Gmail API. The app requests the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify, which allows the app to read and modify messages and send email on your behalf. You can decline authorization or revoke access at any time.

3. Gmail information the app accesses

Depending on the features you use, the app can access message IDs, sender and recipient headers, subject and date headers, message snippets and text content, message labels, mailbox profile counts, and attachment data that you choose to download. It may also create labels, change message labels, move messages to Trash, and send messages when you request those actions.

4. How information is used

Gmail information is used only to provide the mailbox features you choose, such as displaying messages, running Gmail searches, calculating sender counts, exporting a sender list at your request, managing labels, and carrying out message actions. Sender analysis is calculated locally in your browser.

5. Storage and disclosure

This version is a static, client-side website. It does not include an application backend or database. The OAuth access token is held in browser memory while the page is open and is not intentionally stored by this app in local storage, cookies, or an app server. The OAuth Client ID may be saved in your browser's local storage for convenience. Message content and sender statistics are handled in the browser and are not uploaded to an app-operated server by this code.

The site is distributed through a static hosting provider, which may process ordinary website request and security logs. Google processes authentication and Gmail API requests under Google's own policies. The app does not sell Gmail data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

6. Downloads and actions

Sender CSV files and attachments are downloaded to your device only when you choose to export or download them. Email actions such as archiving, labeling, marking read/unread, reporting spam, moving messages to Trash, and sending messages are performed only after you initiate the action. Bulk cleanup requires a separate confirmation.

7. Retention and control

Reloading or closing the page clears the in-memory access token and data held by the app. You can use Settings to disconnect or revoke Google access. You can also review or revoke third-party access in your Google Account security settings. Data already present in your Gmail account remains subject to your Gmail settings and Google's retention practices.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not designed for children under the age required to independently authorize a Google account in their jurisdiction. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

This policy may be updated when app functionality or data handling changes. The updated date above will be changed when revisions are published.
