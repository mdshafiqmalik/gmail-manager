Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Gmail Manager is designed to help you manage your own Gmail account.

Information we access

When you authorize Gmail Manager, the application may access Gmail data required for features you choose to use, such as reading message metadata, searching messages, and moving selected messages to Trash.

How your data is used

Your Gmail data is used only to provide the application's requested mailbox-management features. Gmail Manager does not sell your Gmail data.

Data storage

If you run the application locally, authorization credentials and tokens may be stored on your device. Keep these files private and do not share them publicly.

Third-party services

Gmail Manager uses Google services and the Gmail API. Your use of those services is also subject to Google's applicable terms and policies.

Your control

You can revoke the application's Google account access at any time through your Google Account security settings.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the project's GitHub repository.
